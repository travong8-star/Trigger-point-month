ATTRIBUTION

3D anatomical meshes derived from Z-Anatomy,
licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

MODIFICATIONS
-------------
- Muscle subset extracted from full-body anatomical model
- Converted to glTF 2.0 binary format
- Decimated for web delivery (201,268 triangles)
- Normals computed at runtime to reduce payload

These modified meshes are redistributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
